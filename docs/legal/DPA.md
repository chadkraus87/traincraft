## Preamble — why this document exists and who it protects

This Addendum is usually presented as vendor paperwork. It is not, primarily.

**It is what makes the trainer's own compliance position defensible.** Under the
California CCPA, a disclosure of personal information to a vendor is protected
by the service-provider exemption *only* where a conforming contract exists —
without one, the trainer's disclosure to CoachRhythm may be characterized as a
"sale." Under the Washington My Health My Data Act and Nevada SB370, CoachRhythm
qualifies as a *processor* rather than a *regulated entity* only because it is
contractually bound to act on the trainer's documented instructions. Under the
GDPR, Article 28 requires this contract outright.

In each case the contract is what converts an argument into a defence — for the
trainer at least as much as for CoachRhythm.

## 2. Definitions

- **"Applicable Data Protection Law"** — all privacy, data protection, and
  consumer health data laws applicable to a party's processing of Client
  Personal Data, including the CCPA/CPRA, the Washington My Health My Data Act
  (RCW 19.373), Nevada SB370 (NRS 603A.400 et seq.), the California CMIA, and
  other US state comprehensive privacy and consumer health data laws.
- **"Client"** — an individual about whom Customer records information in the
  Service. Clients do not have accounts and have no direct relationship with
  CoachRhythm.
- **"Client Personal Data"** — personal information relating to a Client that is
  processed by CoachRhythm on Customer's behalf, including **Health Data**.
- **"Health Data"** — Client Personal Data concerning physical or mental health,
  including limitation and injury tags, free-text health detail, training
  history, session notes, body measurements, performance logs, and the exclusion
  rationales generated by the Service. Includes "consumer health data" under
  Washington and Nevada law, "sensitive personal information" under the CPRA,
  and "sensitive data" under other state laws.
- **"Controller"** — the party that determines the purposes and means of
  processing. Includes "business" (CCPA) and "regulated entity" (MHMD, SB370).
- **"Processor"** — the party that processes on the Controller's behalf.
  Includes "service provider" (CCPA) and "processor" (MHMD, SB370).
- **"Subprocessor"** — a third party engaged by CoachRhythm to process Client
  Personal Data.
- **"Security Incident"** — a breach of security leading to accidental or
  unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorized disclosure of, or access
  to Client Personal Data. Excludes unsuccessful attempts and routine events
  (pings, port scans, failed logins) that do not compromise data.

## 3. Roles

### 3.1 Allocation

**Customer is the Controller. CoachRhythm is the Processor.**

Customer determines which Clients to record, what information to record about
them, why, for how long, and what to do with the output. CoachRhythm processes
only as instructed.

This allocation applies under each of: CCPA/CPRA (Customer = business,
CoachRhythm = service provider); Washington MHMD and Nevada SB370 (Customer =
regulated entity, CoachRhythm = processor); other US state privacy laws
(controller/processor); and GDPR/UK GDPR, if applicable
(controller/processor) — noting that the Service is offered only to US customers
under § 2.4 of the Agreement.

### 3.2 Customer's obligations as Controller

Customer represents, warrants, and covenants that it will:

1. comply with Applicable Data Protection Law in its own right;
2. **obtain and maintain all consents, authorizations, and permissions required
   to collect Health Data and to disclose it to CoachRhythm and its
   Subprocessors** — including, where applicable, **separate consent for
   collection and for sharing** under the Washington MHMD and Nevada SB370;
3. **specifically disclose to each Client that a third-party artificial
   intelligence service is used to help generate their training programme**, and
   the categories of information transmitted to it (§ 6.3);
4. provide all required privacy notices to Clients;
5. maintain a lawful basis for all processing;
6. issue only lawful instructions;
7. handle Client requests to exercise rights, and relay to CoachRhythm any that
   require action on CoachRhythm's systems;
8. not enter or continue processing data about a Client who has withdrawn
   consent; and
9. **be solely responsible for the accuracy and currency of the limitation and
   health information it records**, acknowledging that the Service's safety
   filtering operates only on what has been recorded.

**Customer acknowledges that CoachRhythm has no relationship with, and no means
of contacting, any Client**, and therefore cannot obtain consent, provide notice,
or verify identity on Customer's behalf.

### 3.3 CoachRhythm's obligations as Processor

CoachRhythm will:

1. process Client Personal Data **only on Customer's documented instructions**,
   which comprise the Agreement, this Addendum, and Customer's use of the
   Service's features;
2. **not retain, use, or disclose Client Personal Data for any purpose other
   than performing the Services**, or for any commercial purpose of its own;
3. **not sell or share Client Personal Data**, as those terms are defined under
   the CCPA/CPRA, Washington MHMD, Nevada SB370, or any other Applicable Data
   Protection Law, for monetary or other valuable consideration;
4. **not use Client Personal Data to train, fine-tune, evaluate, or improve any
   machine-learning model**, whether CoachRhythm's or a third party's, and will
   contractually require the same of its AI Subprocessor;
5. **not combine Client Personal Data with personal information received from
   any other source**, except as expressly permitted by the CCPA for a service
   provider;
6. **not use Client Personal Data for product analytics, benchmarking,
   aggregated insights, or any derived product**, except in a form that is
   irreversibly de-identified and aggregated such that it cannot reasonably be
   used to identify any Client, Customer, or device;
7. comply with its obligations under Applicable Data Protection Law applicable
   to processors, and **certify that it understands the restrictions in this
   § 3.3 and will comply with them**;
8. notify Customer promptly if it determines it can no longer meet those
   obligations, or if an instruction would in its reasonable opinion violate
   Applicable Data Protection Law (without obligation to provide legal advice);
   and
9. **notify Customer before any change in its role** — including any decision to
   determine purposes or means for its own account, which would make CoachRhythm
   a controller/regulated entity for that processing.

**§ 3.3(4) and (6) are the operative constraints on CoachRhythm's product
roadmap.** Any future feature that uses Customer or Client data for
CoachRhythm's own benefit — cross-tenant analytics, model improvement,
"trends across trainers," published research — would breach them and would
convert CoachRhythm into a regulated entity under MHMD, with the full consent
and authorization regime attaching. Such a feature requires an amendment to this
Addendum and, in all likelihood, fresh Client consent obtained by Customer.

### 6.4 What is disclosed to the AI Subprocessor — specifically

Because this is the disclosure Customers and their Clients care about most, it
is stated precisely.

**Filtering occurs before transmission.** The Service deterministically removes
every exercise contraindicated for the Client's recorded limitations **before**
constructing the request to Anthropic.

**Transmitted to Anthropic:**
- Client goals (free text)
- Client training history (free text)
- Up to the 20 most recent logged performance entries
- Up to the 5 most recent session notes, **verbatim**
- Any additional instructions Customer typed for that generation
- Client equipment list and remote/in-person status
- The already-filtered list of permitted exercises

**Not transmitted to Anthropic:**
- **The Client's limitation and injury tags.** Because filtering precedes
  transmission, the AI Subprocessor is never informed of the Client's diagnosis
  or limitation — only of a pool of exercises already safe for them.
- **The Client's name**, or any other direct identifier.
- Client email address, phone number, or body measurements.

## 7. Security

### 7.1 Measures

**(Annex II equivalent.)** CoachRhythm implements and maintains:

**Access control**
- Tenant isolation enforced in the database by PostgreSQL row-level security,
  such that no Customer can read or write another Customer's data even in the
  event of an application-layer defect
- **Isolation verified by an automated test suite exercising two tenants against
  a real PostgreSQL instance as a non-superuser**
- Ownership checks enforced transitively, so a record cannot be attached to
  another tenant's client
- Authentication and session validation on every request; protected routes
  maintained as an allowlist so that new routes are private by default
- Least-privilege credentials; secrets held only in environment configuration,
  never in source control

**Encryption**
- TLS for all data in transit
- Encryption at rest as provided by the database and hosting Subprocessors

**Operational**
- Rate limiting on generation endpoints, enforced in durable storage
- Input validation and schema enforcement at all API boundaries
- Automated dependency and vulnerability monitoring
- Automated encrypted backups with point-in-time recovery
- Audit records for plan generation, retained per § 9

**Application integrity**
- Deterministic contraindication filtering that **fails closed**: generation is
  refused where a recorded limitation cannot be screened, rather than proceeding
  unfiltered
- An immutable quality-assurance report retained with every generated plan,
  recording which checks ran and their results

### 7.2 What CoachRhythm does not have

Stated expressly so that Customer is not misled, and so that this Addendum
cannot be characterized as overstating CoachRhythm's posture:

- **No SOC 2 report, no ISO 27001 certification, and no third-party penetration
  test** as of the date of this Addendum
- No single sign-on, customer-managed encryption keys, or customer audit-log
  export
- No 24/7 security operations capability

### 7.3 Updates

CoachRhythm may update its security measures provided they do not materially
decrease overall protection.

## 8. Security Incidents

### 8.1 Notification

CoachRhythm will notify Customer of a Security Incident affecting Client
Personal Data **without undue delay and in any event within seventy-two (72)
hours** of becoming aware of it.

`[[DECISION: Breach notification timeline. RECOMMENDATION: 72 hours. Enterprise
customers will push for 24. Do not agree to 24 hours. It is not achievable for a
solo founder who may be asleep, on a flight, or without connectivity, and a
notification commitment that is breached in the same incident that caused the
breach is materially worse than a longer one that is met. If a large customer
insists, negotiate "without undue delay, and in any event within 72 hours" plus
a commitment to a preliminary notification as soon as practicable.]]`

### 8.2 Contents

The notification will include, to the extent known: the nature of the incident;
the categories and approximate number of Clients and records affected; likely
consequences; measures taken or proposed; and a contact point. Where full
information is not available, CoachRhythm will provide it in phases without
undue further delay.

### 8.3 Cooperation

CoachRhythm will take reasonable steps to mitigate and remediate, and will
**cooperate with Customer as reasonably necessary for Customer to meet its own
notification obligations** to Clients and regulators — Customer being the party
with the Client relationship and therefore the party that notifies Clients.

### 8.4 No admission

Notification is not an acknowledgment of fault or liability.

## 9. Retention, deletion, and return

### 9.1 During the term

Client Personal Data is retained for the term, subject to Customer's own
deletion actions and to the automated retention schedule in the Privacy Policy —
which includes rolling deletion of session notes after 18 months and of
performance logs after 24 months.

### 9.2 On termination

- Customer may **export all Client Personal Data** through the Service at any
  time before termination and for **30 days after**.
- **30 days after termination**, CoachRhythm will **permanently delete** all
  Client Personal Data from production systems.
- Deletion from routine encrypted backups occurs on the backup rotation
  schedule; backups are not selectively restored to recover deleted records.
- CoachRhythm may retain (i) irreversibly aggregated or de-identified data, and
  (ii) records required by law or reasonably necessary to establish or defend a
  legal claim — under continuing confidentiality obligations and for no other
  purpose.

## 10. Assistance to Customer

### 10.1 Client rights requests

Client requests go to Customer, not to CoachRhythm. **If a Client contacts
CoachRhythm directly, CoachRhythm will not respond substantively**, and will
promptly inform Customer where the Client can be identified.

CoachRhythm will provide reasonable assistance — through the Service's export
and deletion functionality, and where necessary through manual support — to
enable Customer to respond within the statutory period (generally 45 days under
state comprehensive privacy laws; **30 days** under the Washington MHMD and
Nevada SB370).

### 10.2 Other assistance

Taking into account the nature of processing and the information available to
it, CoachRhythm will provide reasonable assistance with security obligations,
breach notification, data protection impact assessments, and regulator
consultations. CoachRhythm may charge for assistance that is not attributable to
its own act or omission and that materially exceeds routine support.

## 13. Liability, term, and general

### 13.1 Liability

Each party's liability under this Addendum is subject to the **exclusions and
limitation of liability in the Agreement**, and the parties' aggregate liability
under the Agreement and this Addendum together will not exceed the cap in the
Agreement — except where Applicable Data Protection Law prohibits such a
limitation.

Nothing in this Addendum limits a data subject's rights under Applicable Data
Protection Law, or the right of a regulator to enforce against either party
directly.

### 13.2 Term

This Addendum takes effect when Customer accepts the Agreement and continues
until all Client Personal Data has been deleted or returned under § 9.
§§ 3.3, 5, 9, 13 survive termination.

### 13.3 Changes

CoachRhythm may update this Addendum to reflect changes in law, Subprocessors,
or the Service, on **30 days' notice**. Changes will not materially reduce
Customer's protections. If a change does materially reduce them, Customer may
terminate without penalty and receive a pro-rata refund.

### 13.4 Order of precedence

This Addendum prevails over the Agreement and the Privacy Policy as to the
processing of Client Personal Data. Where a mandatory statutory clause set (for
example, the Standard Contractual Clauses) is incorporated in future, it will
prevail over this Addendum to the extent of any conflict.

### 13.5 Governing law

As stated in the Agreement, **provided that** nothing in this Addendum or in the
Agreement's governing-law clause limits the application of any Applicable Data
Protection Law that applies of its own force. **A choice of governing law
governs the contract between the parties; it does not displace consumer
protection or health privacy statutes applicable to residents of other states.**

### 13.6 Severability

If a provision is unenforceable, it is modified to the minimum extent necessary
or severed, and the remainder continues in effect.
